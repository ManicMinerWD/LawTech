User Guide

        

        

          

            
Getting Started

            

              
Dashboard

              
The dashboard provides an overview of your practice. Use the stats cards to see quickly how many clients, cases, and documents you have. Recent items are displayed below the stats.

            

            

              
Clients

              
Register clients here. You can add contact information, company details, industry, and notes. Click on any client to open their detail view.

            

            

              
Cases & Matters

              
Cases are specific legal proceedings. Matters are broader engagements. Associate cases with clients to track relationships.

            

            

              
Knowledgebase

              
Build a searchable library of legal knowledge articles, templates, and precedents. Share with your team for consistent practice.

            

            

              
Documents

              
Upload and manage case documents. Version control is available for tracked documents.

            

            

              
AI Chat

              
Ask the AI assistant questions about your clients, cases, or search the knowledgebase. The AI has access to your data to provide context.
